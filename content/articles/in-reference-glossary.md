# Political terms in plain English

A term is useful when it helps you understand a real question. These short definitions are entry points, not a replacement for the full article or the relevant legal text. Follow the linked explanation when the distinction matters.

## Country

India considered as a political and geographical community; not a synonym for the party in office. 

[Read the explanation](/in/start/country-state-government-party).

## State (in Article 12)

A defined constitutional term for Part III that includes specified governmental institutions and authorities. It is wider than one constituent state. [1](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=37)

[Read the explanation](/in/start/country-state-government-party).

## Government

Often the executive in office: ministers and the administration carrying out public decisions. Identify the institution when the word is being used more broadly. [2](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=65)

[Read the explanation](/in/start/country-state-government-party).

## Political party

A political organisation that may contest elections, govern, support a coalition or sit in opposition. It is not the same thing as the country. 

[Read the explanation](/in/start/politics-without-party-loyalty).

## Parliament

The constitutional institution consisting of the President, Rajya Sabha and Lok Sabha. [3](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=67)

[Read the explanation](/in/parliament/lok-sabha-rajya-sabha).

## Lok Sabha

The House of the People, with representatives elected from territorial constituencies. [3](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=67) [4](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/keps203.pdf#page=6)

[Read the explanation](/in/parliament/lok-sabha-rajya-sabha).

## Rajya Sabha

The Council of States. Its elected state representatives are chosen by elected MLAs using the constitutional indirect-election method. [5](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=68)

[Read the explanation](/in/parliament/lok-sabha-rajya-sabha).

## MP

Member of Parliament. Specify the House and the role when assessing a particular action. [3](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=67)

[Read the explanation](/in/parliament/mp-role).

## MLA

Member of a Legislative Assembly. An Assembly and its state government are not interchangeable institutions. [6](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=106)

[Read the explanation](/in/states/mla-and-mlc).

## MLC

Member of a Legislative Council, in a state where that institution operates. Councils and Assemblies have different composition and powers. [6](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=106) [7](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=119)

[Read the explanation](/in/states/mla-and-mlc).

## Council of Ministers

The executive group of ministers headed by the Prime Minister at Union level. It is collectively responsible to Lok Sabha. [2](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=65)

[Read the explanation](/in/start/country-state-government-party).

## Collective responsibility

The constitutional responsibility of the Council of Ministers to the relevant directly elected House: Lok Sabha at Union level and the Legislative Assembly at state level. [2](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=65) [8](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=104)

[Read the explanation](/in/parties/opposition-role).

## Opposition

The political side outside the governing side that can scrutinise it and offer alternatives. This broad term is different from a formally recognised leadership office. 

[Read the explanation](/in/parties/opposition-role).

## Constituency

An electoral unit that returns a representative under the applicable system. Ordinary Lok Sabha constituency elections return one winner. [4](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/keps203.pdf#page=6)

[Read the explanation](/in/elections/how-seats-are-won).

## First-past-the-post

A constituency voting system in which the candidate with the most votes wins; an absolute majority is not required. [4](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/keps203.pdf#page=6)

[Read the explanation](/in/elections/how-seats-are-won).

## Electoral roll

The list on which an elector must be included for the relevant election. Possession of an identity card is not a substitute for inclusion. [9](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2177191)

[Read the explanation](/in/elections/voter-registration).

## EPIC

Electors Photo Identity Card. Identification and inclusion on an electoral roll are separate requirements to check. [9](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2177191)

[Read the explanation](/in/elections/voter-registration).

## Affidavit

A sworn statement. An election affidavit is a dated disclosure by a candidate, not a judicial finding that every allegation it records is proved. [10](https://myneta.info/)

[Read the explanation](/in/elections/candidate-affidavits).

## Bill

A legislative proposal before enactment. A vote in one House does not automatically mean every required stage has been completed. [11](https://sansad.in/rs/legislation/introduction) [12](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=79)

[Read the explanation](/in/parliament/bill-to-law).

## Act

An enacted law. For practical application, check its commencement provisions and any later changes as well as assent. [11](https://sansad.in/rs/legislation/introduction)

[Read the explanation](/in/parliament/bill-to-law).

## Money Bill

A constitutionally defined category with a special parliamentary procedure. A proposal is not a Money Bill merely because it has a financial cost. [13](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=80)

[Read the explanation](/in/parliament/bill-to-law).

## Panchayat

A rural local-government institution within the applicable constitutional and state-law arrangements. Do not assume identical powers everywhere. [14](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=154)

[Read the explanation](/in/states/union-state-local-powers).

## Municipality

An urban local-government institution whose actual powers must be established under the applicable law, not assumed from its location alone. [15](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=162)

[Read the explanation](/in/local/find-responsible-authority).

## Budget Estimate (BE)

A budget-stage estimate for the specified financial year, not expenditure already completed. [16](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/Key_to_Budget_Document_2026.pdf#page=2)

[Read the explanation](/in/money/estimates-revised-actuals).

## Revised Estimate (RE)

An updated estimate for the specified year. It is still distinct from final reported actual expenditure. [16](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/Key_to_Budget_Document_2026.pdf#page=2)

[Read the explanation](/in/money/estimates-revised-actuals).

## Actual expenditure

Expenditure reported for a completed period under the source’s accounting scope. It does not by itself establish a service outcome. [17](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/Budget_at_Glance/bag1.pdf#page=3)

[Read the explanation](/in/money/read-a-budget).

## Fiscal deficit

In the budget presentation, the gap between total expenditure and receipts other than borrowing, under the stated accounting definitions. [18](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/Budget_at_Glance/bag1.pdf#page=3)

[Read the explanation](/in/money/read-a-budget).

## CAG

Comptroller and Auditor General of India, the constitutional audit institution. An audit observation is not a criminal conviction. [19](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf#page=99)

[Read the explanation](/in/money/audits-and-cag).

## RTI

Right to Information: access to information held by public authorities, subject to the applicable law. It is different from requesting a service repair. [20](https://cic.gov.in/sites/default/files/RTI-Act_English.pdf#page=7) [21](https://aiimsbhubaneswar.nic.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/31-1.pdf#page=5)

[Read the explanation](/in/action/rti-basics).

## CPIO

Central Public Information Officer, the designated information officer in the Central RTI process. A portal nodal officer and the CPIO are not necessarily the same role. [22](https://rtionline.gov.in/faq.php?pageid=c9f0f895fb98ab9159f51fd0297e236d)

[Read the explanation](/in/action/rti-central-or-state).

## Grievance

A complaint seeking attention to a problem, such as a public-service failure. Its appropriate route depends on the subject and authority. [23](https://pgportal.gov.in/)

[Read the explanation](/in/action/choose-a-route).

## Allegation

A claim that something happened or someone did something. The word alone establishes neither truth nor falsity. 

[Read the explanation](/in/crime/case-status-terms).

## Chargesheet

Common terminology for a police report following investigation in the relevant procedural setting. It is distinct from a court-framed charge. [24](https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-04/250884_2_english_01042024.pdf#page=62) [25](https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-04/250884_2_english_01042024.pdf#page=82)

[Read the explanation](/in/crime/case-status-terms).

## Bail

Release from custody under the applicable conditions and bond arrangement. It is not a judgment of acquittal. [26](https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-04/250884_2_english_01042024.pdf#page=2)

[Read the explanation](/in/crime/case-status-terms).

## Discharge

A procedural outcome distinct from acquittal; the cited sessions-trial provisions concern whether there is sufficient ground to proceed before framing a charge. [25](https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-04/250884_2_english_01042024.pdf#page=82)

[Read the explanation](/in/crime/case-status-terms).

## Numerator

The quantity above the division line: what is being counted in a fraction or rate. 

[Read the explanation](/in/evidence/percentages-and-denominators).

## Denominator

The quantity below the division line: the population or total against which the numerator is compared. 

[Read the explanation](/in/evidence/percentages-and-denominators).

## Percentage point

The arithmetic difference between two percentages. A rise from 20% to 25% is five percentage points. 

[Read the explanation](/in/evidence/percentages-and-denominators).

## Reference period

The time the evidence describes. It is not necessarily the year the report was published. 

[Read the explanation](/in/evidence/question-official-data).

## Labour force participation rate

People in the labour force as a share of the relevant population, using the survey’s definitions and reference period. [27](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2246009&lang=1&reg=3)

[Read the explanation](/in/everyday/jobs-and-wages).

## Worker population ratio

Employed people as a share of the relevant population, under the stated survey measure. [27](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2246009&lang=1&reg=3)

[Read the explanation](/in/everyday/jobs-and-wages).

## Unemployment rate

Unemployed people as a share of the labour force, not of the entire population. [27](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2246009&lang=1&reg=3)

[Read the explanation](/in/everyday/jobs-and-wages).

## Primary source

The original record relevant to a claim, such as an order, dataset or recording. Being original does not make it complete or infallible. 

[Read the explanation](/in/reference/how-we-know).

## Corroboration

Additional evidence supporting a claim. Copies of one original story are not independent confirmations. 

[Read the explanation](/in/evidence/check-before-sharing).

## Inference

A conclusion drawn from evidence rather than a fact directly stated or observed. Make the reasoning and uncertainty visible. 

[Read the explanation](/in/start/reasonable-disagreement).

## Conflict of interest

A relationship or incentive that may affect a judgment. Document the relationship; do not treat its existence as automatic proof that every statement is false. 

[Read the explanation](/in/media/assess-a-news-report).
