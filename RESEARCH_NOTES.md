# Research notes and evidence boundaries

Research check date: **13 October 2026**.

This release contains completed prose, not blank research briefs. Its source registry has 22 documents, including 18 official primary documents and four explicitly labelled independent/method sources. The 49 locators identify the actual provisions, passages or tables used. A source URL is not treated as evidence for every imaginable claim about the topic.

## What was actually checked

**Constitution:** official compilation marked as on 1 May 2026; relevant provisions inspected. Article numbers and physical PDF pages are supplied. No current office-holder roster, live constituency boundary map or claim of exhaustive post-compilation legal verification is included.

**Parliament:** official legislative and scrutiny explanations. The Lok Sabha FAQ contains stale historical/current-person fields; no names, salaries or live membership totals were copied from them.

**Budget:** the official 2026–27 document guide and Budget at a Glance table were inspected. The numerical table was checked as a page image, including the unit, row labels and four column headings. The four column totals and growth calculation were checked programmatically.

**PLFS:** numerical and methodological claims use MoSPI's release of 27 March 2026, including its explicit comparability warning. The full report PDF was not successfully retrieved in this research pass. The pack does not imply its hundreds of pages were inspected or that annual 2025 measures describe the current month.

**Road-death example:** both pages of Rajya Sabha Unstarred Question 2820, answered 12 August 2026, were read in text and page images. The three 2024 values are attributed to the Ministry's answer. Its explanation is not represented as an independent audit of all three original data series. The question's premise is not used as a finding.

**Candidate disclosures:** ADR's 6 June 2024 report, original-affidavit provenance and page 6 summary were inspected, including the page image. ADR is independent, not a government source. The cohort is the report's 2024 winning-candidate records, not today's membership or a conviction list. No named case profile is published.

**RTI:** the current Central portal scope and FAQs were inspected. The CIC-hosted Act is an older compilation and the DoPT guide is from 2007, despite a 2025 upload path. Those older sources are used for existing-records concepts, not current exemptions, territorial scope, fees or deadlines. The procedure pages intentionally avoid detailed case-specific legal instructions.

**Local government:** Ministry of Panchayati Raj descriptions establish the purpose of LGD and eGramSwaraj. They do not certify a particular asset's owner, a work's quality or a complete local complaint route. The articles teach how to establish those facts rather than inventing a nationwide operational template.

**Crime vocabulary:** the original BNSS gazette text is used for the stated terminology and savings illustration, not represented as a current consolidated code or individual legal advice. No automated political-motive inference or guilt classification is included.

**Media literacy:** SIFT is cited to its author as a method; Reuters standards are identified as a publisher's own stated standards. Neither is described as a government authority or a guarantee of accuracy.

## Unresolved sources deliberately excluded from new factual claims

- Some Election Commission portals returned a dynamic shell or access error. Official destinations are supplied for readers to follow, but the pack does not pretend the current form UI, document requirements, every affidavit or a particular roll entry was inspected.
- NCRB indexes identified relevant publications, but the underlying 2024 crime tables were not successfully retrieved. No invented all-India political-crime total or supposedly audited NCRB table is included. The road-death comparison uses the inspected parliamentary answer instead.
- A current CAG report landing page was accessible while its full download failed. No finding or alleged misappropriation was inferred from that report title.
- Older report URLs, release leads and source IDs remain available in `roadmap/original-source-leads.json` solely for future expansion. They are not part of the verified-source registry for the 40 articles.

## Publication and review meaning

`status: ready` means finished content with the attached source checks, appropriate qualifications and local consistency checks. It does not mean independently human-reviewed, legally guaranteed or continuously monitored. No per-page human approval is required to import this collection.

A new named allegation, individual legal instruction or unsupported factual addition is not covered by this research. Source it separately and preserve its uncertainty. This restriction does not prevent launching the existing explainers.

## Rights and privacy

This package supplies original explanatory prose, metadata and limited factual transcriptions. It links to, rather than rehosts, source reports. Public accessibility does not establish permission to copy an entire document, photograph, logo or dataset. No source-report PDFs, scraped personal profiles, identity documents or survivor information are redistributed here.
